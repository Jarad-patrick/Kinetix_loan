Overview

Who We Are

Kinetix Exchange Loan is a digital asset lending platform designed to give cryptocurrency holders fast, flexible access to liquidity — without requiring them to liquidate their holdings. We bridge the gap between traditional finance and the digital asset ecosystem by offering collateral-backed loans secured against leading cryptocurrencies.

Founded on the principle that your assets should work for you, Kinetix provides a transparent, technology-driven lending experience. Our platform supports a growing range of digital assets including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), BNB, XRP, and more — enabling borrowers to unlock the value of their portfolio while retaining long-term exposure to the market.

We operate with a commitment to security, speed, and fairness. Every loan is fully collateralised, meaning no credit checks, no lengthy approval processes, and no ambiguity. Our rates are competitive, our terms are clear, and our technology is built to handle your assets with the highest standards of care.

Our Mission

To democratise access to credit by empowering digital asset holders with a simple, secure, and efficient way to borrow against their portfolio — on their terms.

Core Values

  • Transparency — No hidden fees, no surprise rate changes. What you see is what you get.
  • Security — Collateral is held in audited, insured cold storage at all times.
  • Speed — Funds are typically disbursed within minutes of loan approval.
  • Flexibility — Loan durations from 7 days to 12 months with multiple LTV options.
  • Integrity — We act in the best interest of our users and maintain full regulatory compliance.
Process

How It Works

Kinetix Exchange Loan makes borrowing against your crypto straightforward. The entire process — from application to disbursement — is designed to be completed in minutes.

1

Create an Account

Register with your name, username, and a secure password. Verification is fast and your data is protected with industry-standard encryption.

2

Choose Your Collateral

Select the cryptocurrency you wish to use as collateral — BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, CAD, and more. Our platform shows you a real-time estimate of your loan eligibility.

3

Set Your Loan Terms

Choose your Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio, loan duration, and review the applicable APR. Use our built-in calculator to understand your repayment obligations before committing.

4

Transfer Collateral

Transfer your collateral to the designated Kinetix custody address. Once confirmed on-chain, your loan is activated and funds are disbursed immediately.

5

Repay and Reclaim

Repay your loan principal and accrued interest at any time before the due date. Upon full repayment, your collateral is returned to your wallet promptly.

Important: If the value of your collateral falls below the platform's minimum LTV threshold, you may be subject to a margin call. Kinetix will notify you in advance, giving you the opportunity to add collateral or partially repay the loan before liquidation is initiated.

Supported Assets

We currently accept the following assets as collateral: Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), BNB, XRP, Litecoin (LTC), Dogecoin (DOGE), Cardano (ADA), TRON (TRX), CAD Coin (CAD), USDT, and USDC. Additional assets may be added at the platform's discretion.

Interest & Fees

Annual Percentage Rates (APR) are determined by your chosen LTV ratio. Lower LTV ratios attract lower rates, reducing your risk and overall cost of borrowing. There are no origination fees, early repayment penalties, or hidden administrative charges. The only cost to the borrower is the stated interest accrued over the loan duration.
